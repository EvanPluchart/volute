# UX audit — M1, the mixer

Screens: [board.png](m1/board.png) (the flyout on the mock, dark and light, on this machine's real backend, read only, and the Devices view), [main-mock-dark.png](m1/main-mock-dark.png), [devices-mock-light.png](m1/devices-mock-light.png).

## What works

* **One look.** The master on the violet band at the top, then one line per app with its icon, name, fader and figure, then the microphone: the user's sketch, in the logo's colours.
* **Real names and icons.** On the real backend, "Brave Browser", "Apple Music" and "Riot Client" show their own icons and the names their executables give, not file names.
* **Sound you can see.** The live level runs inside each fader's track in amber, the one accent. The meters rise fast and fall slowly, and stay still on silence.
* **Mute where the eye is.** A click on an app's icon mutes it: the icon fades and is struck through in red, the only red on screen. The master and the microphone have their own buttons.
* **Calm when nothing plays.** Apps that stopped playing go a minute later; system sounds stay, at the bottom.
* **The tray icon tells.** It turns to its muted form when the master is muted, its tooltip gives the device and the volume, and the wheel over it moves the master.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A loud level covered the fader's fill: at full level the value was read only from the thumb. | Medium | Fixed: the level is a thinner line through the middle of the track; the value stays readable around it. |
| 2 | Long device names are cut ("Haut-parleurs (Logitech PRO X Wireless G…"). | Low | The full name in a tooltip (Volute's own tooltip, design track). |
| 3 | Apps without a readable name showed their file name in lower case ("hollow_knight"). | Low | Fixed: the first letter is a capital ("Hollow_knight"). |
| 4 | The main window opened at its minimum height. | Medium | Fixed: it opens at 460 × 640. |
| 5 | Keyboard: faders take the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End and the wheel; the tab order through the flyout is not checked yet. | Medium | Check with the keyboard alone before M1 closes. |
| 6 | Changing the default output is in the band (a click on the device's name). Nothing says the name is clickable but the chevron. | Low | Keep; watch whether it is found in a day of use. |

## Not checked by hand

These need a person at the machine, and are the user's to verify (M1's exit is a day of use):

* dragging faders and muting real apps;
* switching the default output (it changes the machine, so no test calls it);
* the wheel over the tray icon, and the flyout closing when focus leaves;
* the tray icon coming back after an Explorer restart.

## Budget (`cargo xtask bench-overhead`, 60 s per state)

| State | Mock | This machine's Core Audio, read only (`--real`) | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open, meters running | 3.2 % CPU, 4.5 MB private | 2.0 % CPU, 5.5 MB private | < 4 %, < 40 MB |
| Idle in the tray | 0.000 % CPU, 3.4 MB private | 0.000 % CPU, 3.8 MB private | < 0.1 %, < 25 MB |

Idle on the real backend includes Core Audio's events from the apps playing meanwhile: they wake the audio thread, which updates its model and sends nothing.
